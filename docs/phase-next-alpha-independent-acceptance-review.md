The external and unique 5B implementations remain separate until one exact pin satisfies all frozen gates and full regression.

Continue offline deterministic and scripted controls. A later bounded local-model experiment needs a separate reviewed authorization, exact source bytes, hidden corpus, prompt/order repeats, resource and cost recording; its output remains research only and cannot sign Findings or authorize writes.
- **4F ownership/fencing: REVISE.** The retained KV lease explains the delay but does not justify lease deletion or preclaim processing. Require a monotonic generation home and rollback anchor, witnessed withdrawal acceptance/freshness, exact target transaction and all-writer barrier, and generation-bound ACK/cursor before implementation authorization. Keep native T4/T5 authoritative and the five-second gate unchanged.
