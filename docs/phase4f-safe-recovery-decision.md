## Proposed v1 denial contract and exact ordering

1. **Enrollment and scope.** An operator pins the deployment, complete target and route roster, canonical affected Evidence identity, eligible withdrawal issuers and scope, native T4 witness set, T5 status authorities, target service identity and rollback anchor. A signed Mesh revocation is only an input. No current reviewed mapping from that revocation to a native Evidence-bundle withdrawal exists; the mapping, canonical bytes, old/new reader behavior and golden vectors require T4/T5 approval before implementation.
2. **Verified status.** A target-side verifier accepts a canonical withdrawal ID and digest only with an authorized issuer and exact scope, independently authenticated T4 witnessed inclusion and continuity, current T5 withdrawal/status assessment, bounded freshness and a noncontradictory view. Its result is `withdrawn`, `unknown` or `conflict` with proof digest, audience and earliest expiry. `unknown`/`conflict` blocks positive authority and may cause a separately labeled local safety hold; neither is recorded as a confirmed withdrawal-specific denial. A coordinator response, broker ACK or journal row alone cannot produce `withdrawn`.
3. **Deny commit.** For `withdrawn`, the enrolled target transaction checks its pinned target identity, current target and owner-generation floors, exact affected plan/Evidence binding and proof. It atomically persists a unique withdrawal tombstone, restrictive target mode, strictly increasing fence and reason-linked audit before replying. Every protected write checks the same tombstone and generation under the same mutation lock at its final commit point. A repeat commits no new logical denial. Restriction is monotone across restarts. Each eligible agent's score requires an independent read of **its own newly committed durable denial**; another agent's hold, transport receipt or pre-existing quarantine does not count.
4. **Transfer.** An authenticated operator/home can fence the old generation without a broker KV claim. The home and external anchor first advance to `preparing(G+1)`. Each pinned writer commits floor `G+1` and quarantine under its write lock, then issues a durable receipt. The authoritative cursor/ACK lane also fences generation `G`. Only after all receipts and fresh exact local approval, target generation, current T4/T5 cut and earliest credential/lease/proof expiry are checked can the home seal `active(G+1)`. An unreachable, partitioned or old-version writer leaves the candidate preparing and positive authority closed. A broker-scoped lease, process death or timeout alone never activates a successor.
5. **Replay.** Record `(deployment, target, withdrawal ID/digest, owner generation, proof digest, denial commit ID, fence)` in a durable ledger. Only after the denial transaction may a current-generation compare-and-swap advance the authoritative cursor. A physical ACK follows that commit and is telemetry. A precommit crash redelivers and commits; a postcommit/pre-cursor crash finds the exact tombstone and advances once; a lost ACK redelivers with no second logical denial. An old consumer ACK may move only isolated old transport state. Journal continuity, T4/T5 proof and target denial are rechecked after a restored cursor.
6. **Recovery.** A restored home, target, denial ledger or cursor below the independent continuity anchor is held. A target reboot requires a fresh higher-generation fence before it can serve positive actions. Correlated rollback of every recovery domain has no local proof and remains outside the accepted deployment model. A hold or tombstone cannot be cleared by old code, an old approval or same-generation restoration. Reopening requires a separate new exact approval and current native cut, and never rolls back a known vulnerable route automatically.

## Authority timeline under faults

The incumbent retains **positive** authority only while its target-home generation, exact approval, credential, lease and native T4/T5 freshness are all current at the target's final locked commit check. It loses authority at the first failing condition there, or when that target commits a higher fence or denial tombstone. A command already committed before the new target fence is ordered before the transfer; an in-flight command cannot commit after it. A partitioned incumbent may remain physically alive, so a new claimant cannot infer its loss from SIGKILL observation elsewhere. Each target enforces its own floor; no successor becomes active across the enrolled deployment until the complete barrier commits and the home seals the higher generation. An unreachable target stays restricted or unproven and blocks activation. Credential expiry cannot be extended by an old session. A stale snapshot cannot lower the anchor floor.

The proposed deny-only path has a different boundary: the target's durable denial takes effect at its serialized commit even while the incumbent still has a broker lease. Subsequent positive writes by that incumbent fail against the tombstone. This does **not** transfer ownership or let the verifier ACK/cursor as the incumbent. If the verifier cannot reach an eligible target, or cannot prove native status, that pair is censored or failed under the frozen oracle; it is not counted as a five-second pass.
