Extra attempts in the original arm and `Evidence.verify` dropping unsuccessful observations are consistent with a transient collection failure. The original records cannot establish the cause. The disposition is a documented, unreproduced availability/provenance miss that stayed fail-closed. Compatibility sharing remains experimental and supplies no write authority.
