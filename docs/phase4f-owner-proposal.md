## Dependency order and contracts to review

1. Independent validator freezes the original failures and healthy controls. Preserve signed Finding 1.0/1.1, native T4/T5, local approval, audit reason codes, old journal order and denied history. Do not edit these shared contracts during the design review.
2. Review a target-owned restrictive hold and its startup recovery path. A serving-path 503 is an observation, not evidence of a committed hold. Specify lock order with the monitor, route proxy and ownership home before implementation.
3. Review a generation-scoped ACK/cursor proposal with exact old/new field examples, broker session proof, home transaction, old-reader behavior, state migration, lost-ACK and restore semantics. Use a separate version if the old consumer cannot reject stale ACKs.
4. Review the native protected writer’s single-plan and transaction contract. The additive B marker hook demonstrates one narrower SQLite path; it cannot be wired as native authority without target, audit, cut and restore proof. Coordinate Trust inputs with T4/T5 owners and independent D.
5. Diagnose critical delivery and test-harness tails with instrumentation. Benchmark paired cases under pinned single-host fixture resources. A physical multi-host and separately administered custody study requires the user’s authorized host inventory and enrollment references before any connection.
6. Integrate one reviewed slice at a time in a new integration worktree. Run focused cross-component checks after each slice, then the full exact-pin and independent adversarial suites. No master merge or distributed writer activation follows from local pass counts.

## Falsifiable acceptance oracles

- **Native proactive hold.** From an enrolled, confirmed three-target fixture, cut ownership with observer traffic withheld. Before any HTTP or health request, read SQLite in a separate read-only transaction. Require rollout and all targets durably restrictive at raised fences. Repeat after kill, restart, unchanged-generation reconnection, status loss and partial rollback. The original Phase 4E observation must still fail on its pinned source.
- **Native ACK/cursor.** With two authenticated broker sessions, delay the old session’s successful ACK response until after generation transfer. Exactly one logical decision persists, the old session advances no current cursor, and a new session replays or confirms the same event without losing a scoped withdrawal. Repeat with restored owner and audit snapshots.
- **Native commit.** Stage marker, receipt and success audit, then change T4/T5 status, generation, expected fence or shortest deadline before COMMIT. The effect, consumed nonce and success audit roll back together. Durable denial or hold remains independently observable. A healthy exact approval succeeds once.
- **Withdrawal ceiling.** Under a fixed admitted ordinary backlog and controlled broker/process faults, measure authority acceptance to every eligible agent’s durable denial and independently observed target hold. Predeclare actors, missing observations, p50/p95/p99, maximum, CPU/RSS, backlog and resource limits. One actor above five seconds keeps the all-agent gate open.
- **Broad suite.** Preserve the 348-case failing XML, diagnose the startup and admission causes with traces, then obtain an exact-pin full suite with zero failures, errors or skips under the declared profile. Repeat the original cases and original native observer-order failure against their historical pins. Do not weaken assertions or silently omit tests.

## Open risks

A current status checked before SQLite’s final commit can expire during blocking I/O. Remote withdrawal admission after the last sampled cut remains a bounded distributed race. A failed disk or stopped target may not persist a proactive hold even if the proxy denies. Generation authority could regress if the home and target anchors are restored together. Critical transport shares host and broker resources with ordinary traffic. A clean local suite would not establish independent custody, authenticated physical multi-host timing or the five-second all-agent bound. These capabilities remain gated until independent evidence closes each condition.
