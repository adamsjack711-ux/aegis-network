## Targeted remediation criteria

Native T4/T5 stays authoritative. The C compatibility review is GO for disposable lab and shadow only, with a reproduced denial regression. A subsequent C experiment needs a distinct pinned policy and a matched test that still reproduces the original behavior. A fresh positive decision must account for every designated denial-lane witness when an accepted withdrawal at any one of them is in scope. The 3-of-4 history quorum alone cannot demonstrate that condition. A compatible conservative remedy can require fresh authenticated observations from all designated denial lanes before using a verified 3-of-4 cut for a positive shadow appraisal. Document the corresponding outage cost.

Define acceptance as durable witness intake of the original, canonical, signature-valid and in-scope revoker record, associated with its pinned role, key, policy, audience and admission time. A matching scoped denial takes precedence over prior positives. Persist permanent denial only after native validation of the revoker authority and exact record scope, with verifiable witness evidence or the established native enrollment path. Untrusted input cannot supply a tombstone. A missing peer, unverifiable receipt, unsupported policy or failed continuity proof creates temporary unavailability. A current nonce or a transport receipt cannot extend the old cut. Distinguish a withdrawal committed before the observer's accepted challenge snapshot from a later race during the bounded expiry interval.

A's adapter proposal must carry original signed bytes and distinguish intake, publication, durable local denial, applicable witness receipts, sealed inclusion and independent status. Replay retains original identities and normal cursor semantics. Each verified proof is bound to its own policy and authority scope. Neither an ACK nor an HTTP or replay sequence number is authority. B's fixture transaction must compare exact local approval, trusted Evidence cut, target identity and expected fence, current generation and the earliest proof, lease, assignment and approval deadline at the mutation boundary. An ownership restore or failed proof retains restrictive state. B's separate lab marker or contract test is the only new permissible write target.

D will independently attempt the original C failure and the repaired case, delayed withdrawal, changed owner and cut, stale approval, rollback, incomplete journals and partial outage. Also preserve the broker partition and pre-intake/post-audit crash controls. The five-second all-agent gate stays open until observed independently across all expected actors.
