# Aegis Trust threat model

## Protected decisions and trusted components

The protected transition is a new locally approved target mutation or authority renewal for one deployment and exact Evidence subject. The coordinator and brokers are untrusted for authorization, completeness, ownership and freshness. They may delay, drop, reorder, duplicate or selectively disclose valid bytes. The agent relies on enrolled keys, the ownership home and target transaction, scoped application signatures, a separately administered Evidence service, an independent archive, and independently administered intake witnesses.

Independence includes identity administrators, signing keys, hosting, deployment pipelines, time sources, DNS and recovery custody. Distinct key IDs establish separate signatures. In deployment, assess independence by shared failure and compromise paths. The prototype provisions all roles on one host for synthetic tests.

## Witness safety and receipt boundary

For interchangeable quorum roles, choose a roster size `n` and threshold `q` with `2q − n > f`, where `f` is the maximum Byzantine operators. Aegis Trust proposes four witnesses and three signatures for admission and coverage, with at most one Byzantine operator per roster. The same roster checks admission and coverage. Any qualifying seal and qualifying receipt intersect in at least two witnesses, including an honest one. That witness persists its obligation before releasing a receipt signature, refuses to seal an omitted obligation, and refuses a divergent prefix. An honest signer has an intact obligation store and a trustworthy epoch clock. Routine membership change needs authenticated common state and old and new quorum approval.

Let a withdrawal be receipted during one-second epoch `e`, with test clock interval `[e, e+1)`. Any qualifying seal through `e` or later must include that record. In epoch `e+1`, the evaluator requires coverage of `e` on a fresh nonce. A stale earlier seal, an omitted leaf, or a missing quorum causes a hold. If the archive supplies the complete prefix, replay applies the denial. A valid operation during `e` can still race the withdrawal because its signed view ends at `e−1`. Production must budget clock skew, intake, seal, delivery, actuator and observation time together.

An event censored before receiving a quorum is outside this guarantee. An authorized revoker can deliberately deny availability within scope. Authenticated semantic lies from a publisher or cooperating eligible verifiers remain possible. A witness can decline coverage, and even a subquorum proposal can pin an honest witness to a losing candidate in the prototype. A partial admission can also block a seal at a witness that recorded it. The prototype favors safety and has no automatic view-change or receipt-abort protocol. A deployment needs reviewed quorum finalization and recovery that carry obligations forward.

## Failure behavior

| Failure | Expected outcome and residual risk |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinator fabricates progress, suppresses events or disappears | Endorsements and the full archived prefix determine coverage. An independent path may supply the cut. Otherwise eligibility holds. Signed assertions say what qualified participants admitted and observed. |
| Witness substitutes its identity or misbehaves | Unknown and duplicate signers are rejected. One Byzantine signer cannot produce a conflicting quorum. With two Byzantine signers, geographically split clients may each accept one cut. A client that sees both conflicting qualifying cuts pins both signed statements and holds. Monitor gossip and rollback-resistant witness state remain deployment requirements. |
| Agent is copied across brokers or kept alive after transfer | The exact enrolled instance key proves possession. One authoritative generation wins each transfer. The target compares generation, holder, assignment and target fence in its write transaction. A compromised home administrator or a bypass around the target transaction can violate this boundary. |
| Revoker retires or is compromised | Historical receipt time and prior grants preserve eligible denials. Backdated self-declared issue time confers no historical authority. Uncertain historical negatives remain restrictive. Compromised active positive keys stop qualifying at their authenticated cutoff. Root quorum compromise requires recovery from independently provisioned trust. |
| Evidence is absent, expired, withdrawn or contradictory | Two current verifier organizations, exact Evidence subject, independent status, and matching replay are required. Negative events survive expiration. Authenticated status disagreement closes the exact cut at the target, including for an already prepared operation. A separate signer can deny service by withholding status. |
| Network partitions or trusted time is lost | Observed loss discards pending local authority. Each new write or renewal needs fresh nonce-bound coverage and status. Already reconciled authority can survive an undetected loss until its hard deadline. The lab uses the earlier of a wall epoch boundary and a one-second monotonic interval. The local model preserves the previously installed restriction and exposes no relaxation action. No offline observation establishes absence of a remote withdrawal or current global ownership. |
| Audit or journal is restored incompletely | The retained external anchor compares the exact checkpoint, status holds, owner, lease and target effect state. Missing state, a stale restore, and interrupted cross-store updates hold. Rolling back both stores or replacing trusted storage administration is outside the lab proof. The anchor commits first, so a crash between stores may require intervention even when the local transaction rolled back cleanly. |
| Target deadline and commit race | The lab checks deadlines under the target lock and just before leaving the transaction. A host can pause inside an actual commit. Production needs a serving-path deadline, restrictive fallback, indeterminate-commit readback, and independently verified coverage at current fences. |

## Out of scope for lab evidence

The prototype uses one target bit and one local transaction domain. It supplies no evidence of live network isolation, SPIRE attestation, certificate rotation, distributed home failover, multi-target barriers, archive durability across administrators, independently observed protection, sustained overload, or fleet availability. There is no external scanning, command invocation, or production enforcement path.

Use authenticated cohort access, minimal event content, separately budgeted withdrawal and replay ingress, and retained audit material in a deployment. Counts, cadence, predictable digests and proof queries can expose sensitive metadata even when payload access is restricted. Lost archives and unavailable quorum paths can halt new actions. Backups require independently retained anchors and evidence of an authenticated handover for witnesses and the ownership home.
