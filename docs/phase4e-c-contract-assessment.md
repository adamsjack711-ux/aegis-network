## Independent review checks

- **Endpoint identity and scope.** Pin the intended service identity or private issuing trust root and exact service identity. Derive caller scope from authenticated transport state reviewed at the endpoint. An untrusted application header carrying an identity string cannot stand in for authentication. Negative fixture cases should include untrusted peers, invalid credentials, the wrong audience or cohort, and attempts to use a read-only role for intake or publication.
- **Receipt propagation and cut bounds.** Record durable admission, quorum receipt, sealed coverage, applicable deadline, and eventual relying-party denial as separate events. A missing fourth operator may be tolerated with the current 3-of-4 policy. Missing required status, a stale pinned cutoff or an unavailable proof holds. Authorization from a previously valid cut is bounded by the existing deadline. Measure receipt-to-denial through the actual seal and independently verify record bytes, the selected cutoff, and denied writes.
- **Restore correctness.** Exercise exact restart, application-only rollback, missing protected state, and separately unavailable pin service with healthy controls. When protected state and its reference can be restored together by one controller, rollback resistance depends on that shared administrative domain. If SQLite state and an independent CAS floor commit separately, exercise an ambiguous interruption between the two and require reconciliation before signing or approving. Preserve revoked digests and scoped denials through rotation and restore.
- **Status protection and capacity.** Bind challenge freshness and exact scope or audience at the relying party. Keep direct status and withdrawal capacity available during ordinary findings. Report two-operator status availability as a different denominator from T4 quorum availability. The current 256-record T5 bound and one-second local refresh budget require explicit restart, capacity and tail-latency evidence before promotion. Separate network delay and real cutoff wait from synthetic clock advancement.

## Release interpretation

Single-host authenticated transport can verify endpoint and role checks with synthetic credentials and real sockets. Separately administered hosts, operational clock error bounds, durable storage custody, long-disconnected recovery through roster changes, and deployed network-to-denial latency require authorized independent infrastructure. C code can supply inputs to the disposable fixture or shadow path. Distributed enforcement remains disabled until the combined validation closes both findings against an exact candidate.
