# Phase 4D architecture

## Accepted integration boundary

The reviewed Phase 3C signed contracts remain the common input. Mesh distributes exact signed bytes, maintains its event, outbox and replay journal, and acknowledges delivery after a durable local decision. Native Evidence verifies materials, worker profiles and in-scope attestations. Rapid independently binds those inputs to local trust, inventory and exact review. The combined candidate requires the existing exact durable Rapid decision, a verified native Mesh cut and independent Evidence endorsement. It also requires an enrolled ownership generation at the target transaction. The only writer controls the fixed disposable lab fixture after explicit exact local approval. Distributed authorization remains disabled. The original simplified Trust evaluator remains a read-only comparison lane. | Component | Trust boundary and failure behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Mesh and broker | Delivery and recovery are durable and at least once. The coordinator or broker may delay, omit or reorder. Their confirmation never establishes history coverage or grants write authority. |
| Independent admission and history (T4) | Admit original scoped signed records outside coordinator control. Commit ordered, dense logical leaves, durable receipts, signed checkpoints and consistency proofs. Coverage must include the applicable receipts by the sealed cutoff. Failed, partial, stale or equivocal coverage blocks new writes. |
| Identity home and target writer (T3) | Pin deployment, transport principal, allowed broker and holder key. Keep one durable generation authority per deployment. Before activating a replacement, persist fences at each enrolled writer. Serialize fence and conditional write in the target transaction. Conflicting or unprovable ownership is unavailable. |
| Evidence status and verification (T5) | Enroll verifiers and status and revocation authorities separately. Require two eligible verifier organizations and exact materials and controls. Independent status applies scoped signed withdrawals and contradiction holds, uses a versioned cut and expires. A signature proves provenance and scope only. A collector or cache cannot override local status. |
| Authorization, observation and recovery (T6) | Bind local approval to the exact finding, Evidence status and history cutoff, owner generation, target fences and deadline. Independently probe all declared marker and benign paths. Unavailable or contradictory evidence removes authority. Safe recovery preserves a restrictive state and distinguishes observed quarantine from unverified recovery. Model-generated text has no authority role. |

## Authority dependencies

The native profile uses the existing Ed25519 library for portable records and the RFC 9162 SHA-256 Merkle tree construction for journal commitment and consistency. A threshold on a checkpoint proves coverage only when sufficiently overlapping independent admission and coverage custodians verify their durable obligations. The fixture uses 3 of 4 in a fixed cohort with at most one Byzantine witness and trustworthy witness persistence. It excludes censorship before quorum admission, collusion outside that bound and compromise of the local target authority. Separate role keys and files in one test process provide simulated role separation. [RFC 9162 tree and proof algorithms](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html#section-2.1)

The identity research recommends SPIRE-issued X.509-SVIDs over mutual TLS, enrolled instance proof of possession and TUF for versioned trust metadata. Native T3 serializes the home generation and all fixture replicas with one SQLite lock and uses signed per-transition intents. Its peer and broker identities come from caller-supplied adapter context. A live ownership service must authenticate that context and prevent generation rollback at its primary and each target adapter. Native T5 appraises original Evidence and short-lived direct attestations from two verifier organizations against both pinned status signers and a complete cohort view. Both models assume configured time and storage custody. The separately imported original Trust prototype uses a full-prefix hash, modeled assignments and a single status signer. Its records remain distinct from the native wire inputs.

Expiration uses the minimum of signed cutoff age, local monotonic budget and grant, Evidence status, plan, ownership and lease deadlines. At each protected stage the fixture rechecks the signed cut, fresh Evidence challenge and exact bindings. It compares the generation within the target write and checks expiry again before commit. Detected isolation invalidates the current gate and stops new writes. A still-valid previous cut can cover an in-flight transition until its fixed deadline, including when a new withdrawal has yet to reach a sealed view. A reconnect or restore needs a fresh complete cut, historical key appraisal and generation reconciliation before fresh approval. Restrictive recovery preserves the observed quarantine. Live long-isolation, clock uncertainty, boot and recovery domains still require a separate validation campaign.
