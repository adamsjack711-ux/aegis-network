# Phase 4F recovery resolution — architecture recommendation

**Decision: REVISE / NO-GO.** This is a design and synthetic-test result at starting reference `8292e531194216a578c32c912d066f98cdbce9ea`. It authorizes no shared contract, runtime ownership, lease, fencing, Trust, target, or ACK/cursor change. The retained 30-second FILE KV lease and five-second all-agent durable-denial gate are unchanged. Distributed autonomous enforcement remains **NO-GO**.

## Recommendation and boundaries

Keep the existing fail-closed lease-expiration baseline for broker ownership. Develop two separately reviewable protocols: **(1) an enrolled agent-owned, pre-broker-claim, deny-only audit path** for independently discovered and verified exact withdrawals, and **(2) a later generation-fenced positive owner-transfer protocol**. A target-side restrictive transaction is useful independently of broker recovery, but it is a target protection result and cannot stand in for each agent's own durable SQLite denial. Neither path is implemented or proven here. Do not force-delete or shorten the lease to improve latency; an old process can be paused, partitioned, or restored and then act on an apparently valid old claim.

The deny-only path is the only candidate that could avoid waiting for broker ownership while preserving the lease. It must use the operator-enrolled canonical agent/store, direct independently authenticated native T4/T5 evidence, exact local inventory and policy, and one atomic agent audit transaction. It has **zero** target-positive, broker ACK, cursor, or successor-owner authority. A separate target supervisor may commit a restrictive hold under the target's own monotonic transaction; it cannot write an agent `revocation_decisions` row or satisfy that metric. Positive recovery requires the later all-writer transfer barrier and fresh authorization. Until both contracts pass review and the frozen oracle, retain the wait and report the deadline failure.

## Threat and failure assumptions

The adversary may control or suppress coordinator and broker traffic, replay or reorder signed envelopes, delay ACKs, present stale but correctly signed views, and resume an old owner after a pause, partition, broker failover, or snapshot restore. Two successors may race. A broker or copied local volume is not an identity or rollback authority. The model allows one faulty native T4 witness under the existing four-operator, 3-of-4 **positive** cut policy; T5 Evidence status has its separately pinned two-operator policy. Cryptographic key compromise, quorum compromise, a compromised operator enrollment or external anchor, and a target that ignores the final transaction check are outside a safe transfer claim and require separate threat treatment. Clock uncertainty, resource exhaustion, inaccessible stores, lost witness views, and missing credentials are explicit failure outcomes, never assumed away.

The proposed deny-only one-witness obligation rule is conditional: the original scoped publisher-signed Revocation 1.1 must be valid, its historical grant and current compromise state must be checked, and a separately administered latest accepted-obligation floor plus complete source/proof must be retrievable. A fresh signature over an **old** head is insufficient. Native T5 reports Evidence status; a Mesh revocation does not automatically make Evidence `withdrawn`. A valid Mesh withdrawal can produce an exact Mesh denial while T5 remains `reproduced`; an Evidence-only withdrawal produces an Evidence hold, not a fabricated Mesh row. Unknown, conflicting, expired, partial, or unavailable views block positive authority and permit only a clearly labeled local uncertainty hold. An untrusted party cannot create an arbitrary withdrawal-specific denial through a Mesh receipt, HTTP response, shadow 4E-C view, or single unanchored witness statement.

## Ownership state machine and barriers

| State | Entry and permitted work | Exit/barrier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `unclaimed` | Canonical process/store enrollment checked; no broker or target-positive authority. | Broker claim may enter `owner(g)`. Reviewed direct native proof may enter `deny_only`, without a broker claim. |
| `deny_only` | Commit exact agent-owned negative audit/tombstone/provenance atomically; target-side supervisor may independently restrict. No ACK, cursor, positive plan or target reopening. | A later owner claim and audit-anchor reconciliation; never implicit promotion from a denial receipt. |
| `owner(g)` | Broker consumption is transport. Positive target writes require current external generation, every writer's fence, exact local approval, plan, target identity, current T4/T5 cut and earliest expiry checked in the final target transaction. | Loss/uncertainty enters `held`; authenticated transfer starts `fencing(g+1)`. |
| `fencing(g+1)` | Independently administered monotonic generation authority commits `g+1` before any successor positive action. Every enrolled protected route/replica and ACK/cursor lane must reject `g`. The old actor may remain alive. | All barriers acknowledged and newly checked approval/Trust permit a **separate** positive authorization. Missing writer or lane stays held. |
| `held` | Restrictive target state and denied/unknown provenance persist. No new positive write. Broker replay may be retried only under the valid generation. | Restore/rejoin proves latest external floors, reappraises Trust, reconciles denials, and obtains fresh approval. |
| `rollback_detected` | Local generation, target fence, audit root or cursor below an independently held floor, or conflicting clone/enrollment. No positive action, cursor advance or claimed on-time denial. | Operator-controlled recovery from verified history and fresh generation; never silently reset a floor. |

The generation authority must be operator enrolled, linearizable for compare-and-swap, durable and monotonic across broker failover, and independently rollback anchored outside broker, agent, target and restorable snapshot domains. It authenticates workload principal, deployment, broker identity and the **complete** protected-writer/consumer roster. Its commit raises a floor; it does not grant a protected write. Every writer checks that floor inside the mutation transaction; every route and replica needs the same barrier. A missing or old-version writer, unreachable target, unavailable anchor, conflicting successor, or unretired old credential prevents completion. The successor cannot use a dead-PID observation or KV revision as a substitute. Mixed-version migration must revoke old executable/file/target/consumer credentials outside the SQLite schema before enabling profile-2 code; an additive table alone does not exclude old writers.

## Revised contract proposal for Main Astra review

These are interface obligations, **not adopted wire formats**. Exact canonical bytes, domains, schemas, trust rosters, error codes, migrations and negative vectors require native and independent review before implementation.

| Proposed record/transaction | Authority and required binding | Commit and rejection rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NativeObligation/Floor.v1` | Native T4 witness validates original signed revocation, scope/grant at admission, target triple, context/roster and sequence; independent anchor keeps exact source bytes, ordinal/root and latest accepted floor. Challenge nonce, complete dense pages, terminal proof and continuity bind discovery without a coordinator event ID. Native T5 supplies separate current Evidence scope/status. | Witness ledger append precedes outbox; anchor append precedes completed receipt. Crash/lost reply replays idempotently. Stale signed head, missing page, absent latest floor, contradiction or source mismatch is `unknown`, never positive authority or a scored exact denial. 3-of-4 sealed T4 remains required for positive history. |
| `AgentAuditDenial.v2` | Operator-enrolled canonical agent/store/host, exact Finding/Revocation/Evidence map, local inventory/policy/plan snapshot, signed source, native acceptance and current floor, T5 result, earliest expiry, external audit high-water. | One SQLite transaction writes the existing `revocation_decisions` row, `revoked_targets` tombstone, proof provenance and next audit hash. Same-ID/different-bytes aborts. External high-water later anchors continuity; commit-before-anchor stays negative-only. Preclaim path never touches broker ACK/cursor or target-positive state. |
| `TargetRestriction.v1` | Target ID/generation/fence, exact proof or uncertainty reason, current target mode, local restrictive capability and prior tombstone, serialized with **all** protected writes. | Monotonically raise fence and durable restrictive mode in one target transaction. Only exact accepted proof may be reported as withdrawal-specific; uncertainty is a hold. No positive reopening from stale snapshot or old approval. This result is independent of agent SQLite denial. |
| `OwnerTransfer.v1` | Operator-authorized claimant, external compare-and-swap generation/root, authenticated complete writer/route/replica roster, consumer lanes, target fences and old credential retirement. | Raise generation before successor authority; collect every writer/lane deny-barrier receipt. Missing receipt leaves positive state closed. Subsequent positive action still needs new exact approval, current 3-of-4 T4 cut, current T5 and expiry at protected commit. |
| `OwnedCursor.v2` | Agent, store, stream, broker, owner generation, consumer epoch, sequence, event/decision/proof digests, audit high-water. Separate ordinary and critical floors. | Advance current-generation authoritative cursor only **after** exact durable agent denial. ACK follows; redelivery after lost ACK is idempotent. Old-session ACK may alter broker transport metadata but never this floor. Physical ACK fencing needs generation-specific consumers with server-enforced ACL retirement or a reviewed gateway; an application check alone does not fence JetStream ACK. |

Crash order for an exact withdrawal is: native witness/anchor durable admission and discoverable floor → agent verification against current T4/T5/local snapshot → atomic SQLite negative decision → optional external audit anchor update → current-generation cursor compare-and-swap → broker ACK. Kill before local commit leaves no denial and requires replay; kill after commit/before cursor returns the same logical denial and then advances the cursor; kill after cursor/before ACK redelivers harmlessly. A late old-session ACK cannot erase the signed source or authoritative floor. An anchor/store mismatch enters `rollback_detected` and cannot turn a restored positive decision into authority.

## Competing approaches and five-second feasibility

| Approach | Benefit | Safety / gate disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retain 30-second lease expiration | Existing fail-closed broker identity behavior; no early-transfer race introduced. | Measured crash path misses five seconds. Keep as baseline until a reviewed alternative passes. |
| Shorten/delete lease or let Mesh intake bypass claim | Potentially lowers a local timestamp. | Reject: paused or partitioned old owner, cross-broker clone and late ACK are not fenced; Mesh audit is not native T4/T5 proof. |
| Target-side deny-only hold | Can protect a reachable enrolled target independently of broker availability. | Pursue as a separate target safety metric; cannot satisfy each agent's SQLite denial. Unknown hold cannot be counted as accepted withdrawal. |
| Enrolled agent-owned preclaim denial | Can bypass the broker wait for the **negative audit only** while keeping lease semantics. | Preferred research prototype, conditional on native latest-floor discovery, exact mapping, canonical store and external audit anchor. Agent/store/T4/T5 unavailable for >5 seconds makes the hard per-agent gate impossible for that pair. |
| Generation-fenced early successor | Could recover positive owner service sooner. | Defer. Requires external generation, all-writer/route and consumer barriers, credential retirement, rollback proof and fresh approval/Trust. It is not needed to commit a negative audit and is not a five-second denial proof. |

There is no unconditional five-second guarantee under unbounded process death, partition, witness/anchor outage or inaccessible canonical store: the earliest agent-owned commit must occur **after** those resources return. If a required outage lasts more than 5,000 ms from the frozen submission origin, the pair cannot pass. Keep it failed or explicitly censored under the predeclared oracle; do not relabel a target hold as an agent result, reset the clock at rejoin, or weaken the requirement. The path's end-to-end cost also includes admission/anchor, direct discovery, T5, store opening and SQLite commit; none is yet measured at fleet scale. Consequently feasibility under the *frozen* fault matrix is unproven and may be impossible for particular fault durations. The status remains NO-GO.

## Independent test matrix and reproduction

VAL-D/VAL-R's frozen release oracle is **3 enrolled agents × 2 withdrawals × 3 fresh repeats × 4 fault arms = 72 targeted pairs**, plus **18 healthy broker-restart pairs**, on one exact integrated pin. Every fault run has **1,000 pending ordinary findings**. Arms: pre-intake SIGKILL plus broker restart, partition/rejoin, lost ACK/replay, and stale owner/competing successor. Before injection declare agent/store/target roster, signed event IDs, eligibility, isolation/censor rules and two clocks: independent submission and witnessed durable admission. Independently read each agent's canonical SQLite denial/provenance through another connection **before** observer traffic. For each eligible pair require conservative upper bounds ≤5,000 ms from **both** clocks, with no omission; also report restored-connectivity latency, target restriction, ACK and cursor separately. Report attempted/eligible/passed/failed/inconclusive/skipped/censored, each pair's maximum and resource use. Physical multi-host custody and rollback anchors remain separate deployment gates.

Within that matrix independently inject forged/wrong-scope revokers, publisher sequence conflict, wrong Finding/Evidence digest, a fresh signed old T4 head, withheld/malformed pages, partial anchor write, one faulty/withholding witness, T5 withdrawal/contradiction/unavailability, coordinator suppression, old profile writer, competing claims on two brokers, paused old owner, old-session ACK, duplicate/reordered replay, stale agent/target/anchor snapshots, interrupted target transaction, partial writer rollout and all nine declared protected routes. Expected failures are restrictive holds with **no** unauthorized positive write, no false exact denial, no premature cursor, no resurrected route and no hidden denominator. A missing writer/barrier or required authority is an explicit NO-GO state.

The inert [synthetic model](../tests/recovery_phase4f_resolution/test_protocol_model.py) pins the raw diagnostic hash and exercises target-hold/agent-audit separation, two successors, partial writer barrier, fresh positive authorization, three crash positions, lost/old ACK, stale snapshot and >5-second outage. Run `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -m unittest discover -s tests/recovery_phase4f_resolution -v` from this worktree. Result at this pin: **6 tests passed**. These abstract transitions falsify unsafe shortcuts; they do **not** validate signatures, native T4/T5, actual JetStream fencing, SQLite durability, target hardware, timing, or the unrun 72+18 oracle. The original raw traces and Main Astra REVISE decisions remain the reproduction authority.
