# Phase 4D T5 · Independent Evidence status

This lab prototype addresses 4C-04, the finding that the Evidence collector and relying ledgers shared an administrative trust boundary. It starts at the Phase 4C review commit `52a4dc43f129e1087fb29b3410dcda389f324c1b` on `feat/phase4d-t5-evidence`. The Phase 3C writer and existing signed Evidence formats are unchanged. The T5 module exposes evidence for a local decision and carries no actuator or production integration. [Phase 4C finding](phase4c-adversarial.md#4c-04--high--evidence-enrollment-and-withdrawal-share-an-administrative-boundary)

The design follows Aegis Trust's independently governed role grants, fresh nonce-bound coverage, durable relying floors, and separation between checkpoint content and freshness. It keeps Phase 3A's signed manifest, worker controls and two-organization threshold. Phase 4B's portability index can locate candidates. A compatibility result supplies no votes for a different signed manifest in T5. The prototype uses two mandatory status operators and bounded complete snapshots. The Aegis Trust production proposal uses 3-of-4 operators and verifiable admission and coverage proofs. ## Roles and trust

| Role | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher and coordinator | Publish properly scoped signed manifests and route untrusted payloads. The same administrative domain may fill both roles. |
| Verifiers | Test the exact signed bundle with locally enrolled worker, environment, check and oracle pins. Each signs its own observation after its own worker run. Two distinct enrolled verifier organizations must agree for a conclusive result. |
| Revoker | Authenticates and submits a scoped withdrawal targeting an exact bundle, attestation, or verifier public-key digest. Holds separate custody from publishers, verification, status and local enforcement. |
| Status operators | Each validates signed record admission, durably retains its own view, and signs a fresh commitment to the complete recorded set. Both commitments are required. Status signers have no appraisal vote. |
| Local relying agent and enforcement owner | Provision trust, determine local target inventory and eligibility, reconstruct Evidence, approve the exact plan and control the writer. A positive Evidence assessment is one input to that decision. |

The policy pin commits the stream, cohort, verifier, revoker and publisher grants, witness roster, eligible worker profiles, and protocol bounds. Enrollment rejects reused keys and prohibited organizational overlaps. A trusted authority must substantiate the organizations and administrators through separately controlled credentials, stores and services. A signature authenticates the statement and its enrolled origin. It cannot establish that the signer performed a test or that the administrator is independent.

The coordinator can withhold or reorder messages and may hold a publisher key. Under the prototype trust assumptions it cannot replace the two independent verifier organizations, the two status endorsements, or local approval. An update durably received by either honest status operator before its endorsement blocks a matching old cut. That requires direct intake and access to the pinned operators outside coordinator custody. If a record reaches neither operator, the protocol has no knowledge of that record. A joint partition blocks fresh eligibility. If both status operators attest to a false omission from the same view, a fresh reader without a prior floor has no independent way to detect it.

## Evidence-state protocol v1

1. **Publish and verify.** Intake identifies the expected signed bundle digest and its claim. The bundle binds the subject, configuration, dependency lock and recipe by SHA-256 and size, and pins the environment, check, and oracle. Each verifier validates the complete package against its own policy, runs the enrolled inert marker worker and evaluates positive and negative controls. It signs the exact signed bundle SHA-256 in the existing Evidence attestation format, with an observation of `reproduced`, `not-reproduced`, or `inconclusive`. The observation expires no later than 60 seconds from issuance, the manifest expiry, or either applicable grant deadline. A worker failure or unhealthy controls yields a signed inconclusive result when the run interval remains valid.
2. **Admit independently.** Publishers, verifiers and revokers send the original signed bytes to each status operator through independent intake. Each operator authenticates role, key, scope and validity at the time of admission. Observations require a previously admitted signed manifest. `StatusWitness.admit` returns the exact wire digest only after SQLite commit, with exact retries idempotent. This API acknowledgment is local to the prototype. A deployed collector needs authenticated, durable admission receipts. Treat an authenticated revocation admitted during its window as a permanent denial of its exact target under the pinned policy. A new operator cannot first admit an expired withdrawal based on the envelope's self-declared time.
3. **Challenge and endorse.** `RelyingStatus.evaluate` validates material bytes and caller-supplied expected bundle, claim and environment pins. It generates a 256-bit random nonce and a challenge bound to the audience, policy pin, cohort, exact signed bundle, claim and material scope. The scope digest commits the manifest fields after removing issuer, key ID, record ID, issue time and expiry. It includes all material pins, family, environment, check and oracle. Each operator independently signs its head, exact challenge SHA-256, signer ID and validity. An endorsement is usable only within the challenge window and strictly before expiry. The lab window is 10 seconds; T6 should apply a tighter stage deadline as described below.
4. **Verify continuity and completeness.** The relying agent requires one current signature from each pinned operator on the same head. Revision is the count of unique original wire records. Root is SHA-256 of `AEGIS-EVIDENCE-STATUS/v1<NUL>archive<NUL>` followed by their raw 32-byte digests in sorted order. It checks the full archive against the head, including count, unique digests and signature, validity and scope against the pinned trust epoch. Both operator signatures vouch that the records passed admission under the applicable grants. The relying agent persists the accepted digest set as a floor. A certified strict subset is stale. A complete certified cut that replaces accepted history triggers a durable hold. Unmatched heads signal divergence and prevent appraisal until they agree. The lab archive is limited to 256 records of at most 8,192 bytes each. If any otherwise admissible update would overflow the bound, that operator durably stops endorsing.
5. **Appraise Evidence.** Recompute eligible observations from the complete cut. An authenticated, admitted reproduced and not-reproduced pair over the same material scope creates a durable contest, including across signed manifest reissue and natural expiry. A revoked exact bundle, revoked supporting vote or key, or expired supporting vote cannot supply authority. A qualified alternative verifier quorum can still support the exact bundle unless the bundle itself is withdrawn or the scope is contested. The reader returns an outcome, reason, and where available a head and the supporting or conflicting attestation digests. A review binding exists only for a reproduced result. Its stable digest covers policy, audience, scope, head, positive outcome and qualifying attestation digests. A fresh challenge can renew freshness without changing the binding. Any change to the full cohort cut changes the binding, including an unrelated record. The independent Evidence deadline is the earliest endorsement or selected attestation expiry.

The new messages use the repository's compact sorted ASCII JSON encoding and strict canonical decoding. An endorsement signature is Ed25519 over `AEGIS-EVIDENCE-STATUS/v1<NUL>endorsement<NUL>` followed by the canonical endorsement body. The wire envelope holds the body and lowercase hex signature. The challenge is created by the relying caller and can travel through an untrusted relay. The signed response binds its exact bytes. This prototype models the archive as a tuple of original byte strings and leaves transport framing to T6. [Protocol schemas](../src/aegis/evidence/status_protocol.py) · [Witness and appraisal](../src/aegis/evidence/status.py)

| Condition | Evidence state and local effect |
| --- | --- |
| Replicated, current positive quorum | `reproduced`, review binding and short deadline available for local appraisal |
| Replicated, current negative quorum | `not-reproduced`, scoped evidence of the observed negative result |
| Failed controls or insufficient quorum | `inconclusive`, no review binding |
| Endorsement absent, replayed, malformed, expired or divergent | `inconclusive`, no review binding |
| Archive record omitted, altered, duplicated or substituted | `inconclusive`, no review binding |
| Scoped withdrawal, expired evidence, historical contradiction, or certified replacement history | `inconclusive`, no review binding. Admitted withdrawals, contested history and accepted continuity floors survive restart. |

A missing response or archive is compatible with a fault, delay, or withholding. The denial holds without attributing intent. A record arriving just after endorsements can invalidate the next fresh appraisal. It cannot retroactively change an already issued short-lived proof. The integration must bound that interval and recheck at each protected operation.

## T6 integration

1. **Use a reviewed integration checkout.** Apply the T5 commit on the Phase 4C-based integration candidate. For Phase 4B compatibility, apply its reviewed commit `e3a526d4109f0879a8da649c9c9b680bfc82c59a` before T5 and reconcile the shared status log. Preserve Phase 3A wire bytes and Phase 3C behavior. Use the distinct T5 message namespace in a shadow evaluator first and then in the disposable Phase 4D writer.
2. **Provision real separation in the lab.** Provision one `StatusWitness` per independently administered signer and a separate `RelyingStatus` store per deployment and cohort. Generate keys in each owner's custody. Pin the matching policy from governed local configuration and explicitly enroll the intended worker profiles. Deliver each manifest and direct verifier observation to both witnesses. Give revokers independently reachable intake. Exercise authenticated cohort-scoped transport and bounded authenticated intake, reading both status operators via routes outside coordinator control. A direct intake error, missing endorsement, or store/recovery error must deny new or expanded writes. Keep status and withdrawal intake available under ordinary load.
3. **Resolve scope locally.** At the `EvidenceGate` boundary, run `phase3c.check_bundle` against the locally authenticated finding. Resolve trusted target inventory and compare its exact materials, configuration, runtime, dependencies, recipe, check and oracle pins to the manifest. For the prototype call `RelyingStatus.evaluate(bundle, expected_bundle_sha256=…, expected_claim_sha256=…, expected_environment_sha256=…, fetch=…)`. Source those pins from the locally appraised finding and inventory. The transport adapter gathers one endorsement per operator and a complete archive for the endorsed cut. Enforce the 10-second protocol window and a local request deadline compatible with the Phase 3C one-second Mesh freshness budget. Document authenticated channel, body limit, timeout, and time source. Preserve the original proof and resulting reason for the audit.
4. **Bind and recheck.** During explicit plan preparation require `outcome == "reproduced"` and use `binding_sha256` in the plan, along with the exact finding, target and inventory, agent identity and generation, Mesh checkpoint, fence, operation and deadline. At each privileged stage, including canary, each peer write and any authorization renewal, draw a new challenge and require the exact locally approved binding. Validate the earliest current Evidence, Mesh, trust, lease and local approval expiry at transaction commit. Recheck the last-known denial and serialize its promotion with the local write. Convert wall time to a conservative monotonic deadline using measured clock bounds. Never use a cached positive or a compatibility assessment as fallback. An Evidence denial must still permit the existing restrictive recovery path when local policy requires it.
5. **Qualify the boundary.** Exercise both initial and post-canary withdrawal delivery through one witness with the coordinator suppressing relays, plus partial and conflicting status, signer or collector loss, expiry, and lost or stale storage. Capture signed proof bytes, durable floors, denial and approval digests, target fences, and independent effect readback. Preserve known denials across downgrade and recovery. Keep production enforcement gated on actual separate-custody validation, availability measurements and integration approval.

The prototype pins a static trust epoch and uses full-cohort replay and SQLite floors. It does not implement a network service, operator consensus or key rotation, verifiable archive recovery after a missed admission window, a safe release process for contested scopes, or protection against restoring an older local database alongside stale signers. A deployment needs externally anchored floor recovery, authenticated intake receipts, protocol for trust epoch changes, and independent operator validation. The bounded archive also requires a safe rollover mechanism before a longer-running pilot. No production custody, network latency, enforcement timing or mitigation efficacy is established here.

## Reproduce

Run from this worktree with the project's Python dependencies. The complete suite needs local Unix and loopback sockets for inherited fixtures.

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider tests/phase4d_t5
PYTHONPATH=src python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/benchmark_phase4d_t5.py --warm-samples 100 --cold-samples 20 \
  --sizes 3 32 128 256 --output benchmarks/phase4d-t5-evidence.json
```

The tests use ephemeral role keys, separate SQLite stores and child worker processes. They exercise all three outcomes with real synthetic controls, publication and legacy-assessment substitution, two keys in one verifier organization, lost, duplicate and tampered endorsements, omitted and substituted archive content, expired status and evidence, all three revocation scopes, durable contest and rollback, and local review binding. Values and provenance are asserted at the public prototype entry point. [Focused test cases](../tests/phase4d_t5/test_independent_status.py)

For the combined handoff, apply the reviewed Phase 4B source before running the focused tests and `tests/test_phase4b_evidence.py`. The portability-specific test then checks that a target compatible with a reproduced source still needs two direct target observations. The Phase 4C base has no portability fixture and skips this case.

On 2026-11-19, the full Phase 4C-based suite passed 157 tests and skipped the Phase 4B-only case, with the inherited Starlette/httpx deprecation warning. An isolated checkout of Phase 4B at `e3a526d` with the T5 modules and tests overlaid passed all 54 focused Phase 4B and T5 cases, including the portability test. These runs used Python 3.15.2. The inherited full suite exercised disposable local sockets.

## Measurements

The benchmark verifies the expected outcome and Evidence provenance throughout. Warm runs use two pre-produced observations and a file-backed legacy reader as a baseline. The independently witnessed path measures the full in-process appraisal, including two serial endorsements from separate SQLite stores, retrieval of a single copy of the archive for the relying agent, and signature reappraisal. Padding through 256 records uses unrelated signed manifests. Cold arms start with empty preprovisioned stores, include both isolated subprocess runs and alternate arm order. The withdrawal runs measure direct durable intake and immediate reappraisal on the same host. Counts include completed and checked samples only. Network, administrative separation, actor queueing and local writer or operator review are outside these timings. [Benchmark source](../scripts/benchmark_phase4d_t5.py) · [Raw samples and environment](../benchmarks/phase4d-t5-evidence.json)

The 2026-11-19 run used Python 3.15.2 on macOS arm64. Each warm row used 100 samples. Payload size counts the two endorsement envelopes and one complete archive, without transport framing.
