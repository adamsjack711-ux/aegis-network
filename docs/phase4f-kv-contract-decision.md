## Required revision before an implementation decision

1. Name the failure-independent, monotonic owner-generation authority, its authenticated claimant, its persistence across broker failover, and its rollback-resistant restore anchor. Show how every incumbent protected writer is excluded before a successor obtains positive authority. A controller death observation, broker TTL or KV revision alone is insufficient under partition or pause.
2. Specify versioned, exact-scope native T4/T5 withdrawal acceptance, witnessed inclusion, quorum/receipt, freshness, contradiction and revocation precedence. A Mesh HTTP response, journal row or broker ACK cannot be converted into Trust status. The denial rule must not let an untrusted party impose arbitrary global holds.
3. Define a target transaction that binds current owner generation, exact local approval, target identity and generation, plan, Evidence/Trust cut, earliest expiry and durable denial tombstone. Every protected writer and every route must perform the final check. Unknown or unavailable status blocks new positive writes; a durable hold cannot be cleared by old code, a replayed approval or snapshot restore.
4. Define an authoritative generation-bound cursor compare-and-swap committed only after the exact denial, with crash-safe idempotent replay. A late old-session ACK may affect broker transport metadata but cannot advance the authoritative recovery floor or authorize a write. Specify lost ACK, pre-commit death and post-commit/pre-cursor death handling.
5. Specify an all-writer transfer barrier and mixed-version migration. Unreachable or old-version targets stay held and ineligible. Positive recovery requires a fresh higher-fence approval and current Trust cut; a safety hold is not a scored withdrawal denial.

The existing 30-second fail-closed behavior remains until this protocol and migration are reviewed. The five-second **all-agent** deadline is unchanged and failed. Do not shorten or force-delete the KV lease, process critical traffic under an old owner, or promote native Trust from transport receipts.
